Quick Summary

What should users understand before using TutorServices?

TutorServices helps students, parents and tutors communicate about possible tuition arrangements. Users must provide accurate information, communicate respectfully and confirm the tutor, schedule, fees, payment terms, learning mode and safety arrangements before regular classes. Registration or enquiry submission does not guarantee approval, a match, availability, leads or academic outcomes.

Plain-English scope: These Terms describe the website and services currently presented. They do not create promises, disciplinary procedures or payment systems that TutorServices does not actually operate.

By accessing the TutorServices website, submitting a form or using a contact option provided on the site, you agree to use the website responsibly and follow these Terms. If you do not agree, you should not submit information or use the website to arrange tuition.

Parents or guardians should manage enquiries for younger children where appropriate. A person submitting information for someone else should have permission to provide the relevant contact and learning details.

Services

TutorServices facilitates tuition enquiries and possible tutor-student introductions

The website describes learning options and allows users to submit relevant requirements or tutor profiles.

For students and parents

Users may request home, online, offline, one-to-one, group, academic, language, computer, hobby or exam-related learning support. Availability depends on the exact requirement and current tutor options. A website page describing a service does not guarantee that a suitable tutor is immediately available.

For tutors

Tutors may submit professional details for consideration when relevant student enquiries arise. TutorServices may review submitted profile information for fit, but registration is not an employment offer and does not guarantee approval, enquiries, students, assignments or income.

Matching support

Potential matching may consider class, board, subjects, level, location, learning mode, timings and fee expectations. A suggested introduction is not a guarantee that the tutor and student will be suitable for each other. Both parties should discuss the arrangement before continuing.

TutorServices and tutors may provide guidance, explanations, practice and progress support. No marks, ranks, admission, examination selection, fluency, employment or improvement timeline is guaranteed. Results depend on many factors beyond a website or tutor's complete control.

User Responsibilities

Students, parents and tutors must use the service honestly and respectfully

All users share responsibility for clear communication and safe, lawful arrangements.

Provide accurate information

Do not submit false names, qualifications, experience, requirements, locations, availability or contact details. Update important information when it changes.

Communicate respectfully

Avoid abusive, discriminatory, threatening, misleading or inappropriate communication. Keep interactions connected with the tuition enquiry or arrangement.

Follow applicable rules

Do not use the website or contact information for unlawful activity, harassment, impersonation, fraud, copyright infringement or unauthorised data collection.

Protect personal information

Share only necessary information, supervise arrangements involving minors and do not publish another user's private details without appropriate permission.

Before regular tuition begins

  1. Confirm the subject, class, board and learning goals.
  2. Discuss qualifications, teaching approach and suitability.
  3. Agree on location or platform, timings and session duration.
  4. Confirm fees, payment timing and cancellation expectations.
  5. Establish appropriate safety and guardian communication.
Tutor Registration

Submitting a profile permits consideration, not guaranteed assignments

Tutors should provide genuine qualifications, experience, subject expertise, boards, class levels, teaching mode, location, fee expectations, availability and resume information. TutorServices may request clarification or additional information where appropriate.

Profile review is explained on the Tutor Verification Process page. TutorServices does not claim universal police or identity background verification for every tutor. Profile approval does not guarantee student leads and can never substitute for the family's final assessment.

Tutors are expected to follow the Tutor Code of Conduct, including professional communication, preparation, punctuality, confidentiality and appropriate boundaries with learners and families.

Fees and Availability

State preferences clearly and confirm final terms

The tutor form allows hourly, monthly or per-course preferences and includes the label Preferred fee (Per hour in Rupees) for hourly expectations. A submitted amount is a preference, not an automatic final agreement.

Before teaching begins, tutors and families should confirm the final fee, session duration, frequency, payment method, due dates, travel expectations, missed-class arrangements and whether any demo or introductory session is free or paid. TutorServices does not publish one universal payment process for every arrangement.

Student Enquiries

Tutor matching depends on the requirement and available profiles

A complete enquiry improves relevance but does not guarantee a match within a fixed time.

Requirement details

Parents and students should submit the correct class, board, subjects, city, preferred mode, timings, fee preference and learning goal. Special academic or accessibility needs should be explained appropriately without sharing unnecessary sensitive information.

Availability and location

Home tuition depends on a tutor being available within a practical travel area. Online tuition can widen access but still depends on subject, level, language and schedule. TutorServices cannot promise coverage in every locality or for every specialised request.

Demos and introductions

A demo or introductory session may be offered in some cases, but it is not guaranteed for every enquiry. Users should confirm the session duration, purpose and payment status in advance and use it as one part of their suitability assessment.

Changes or concerns

If an introduction is unsuitable, contact TutorServices with the specific concern. Another match may be considered subject to availability, but immediate replacement is not guaranteed. Users should communicate concerns factually and protect private information.
