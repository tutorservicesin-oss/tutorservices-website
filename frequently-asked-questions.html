Quick Answer

How does TutorServices help students and tutors?

TutorServices connects students and parents with tutors whose submitted subjects, classes, boards, learning mode, location, schedule and fee expectations are relevant to the enquiry. Learners can request home, online, offline, one-to-one or group support. Tutors can register a profile for consideration when a suitable requirement becomes available.

Important: Availability, demos, tutor matching and final arrangements depend on the specific requirement. TutorServices does not guarantee marks, ranks, admissions, fluency, employment, enquiries or an immediate match.

Before requesting a tutor

  • Know the class, board, subjects and learning goals.
  • Share the locality or choose online learning.
  • Decide suitable timings and a realistic fee preference.

Before confirming lessons

  • Discuss teaching experience and lesson approach.
  • Confirm fees, schedule, cancellation and payment terms.
  • Assess a demo or introductory session where offered.

Questions about requesting and starting tuition

These answers explain the enquiry, matching and confirmation process without promising outcomes that depend on the student, tutor or current availability.

Complete the student registration form with the learner's name, parent details, mobile number, email, board, class, required subjects, preferred mode, city, timing and fee preference. Accurate details make matching more relevant. Submitting an enquiry starts the review process; it does not automatically confirm a tutor or regular class schedule.

Matching considers the student's class, board, subjects, academic goals, home or online preference, locality, schedule and expected fee structure. Available tutor profiles are compared with those details. A relevant match still requires discussion because teaching style, communication and learner comfort cannot be established from form information alone.

Academic support is available from Class 1 through Class 12, subject to tutor availability. The class-wise tuition hub explains primary, middle, secondary and senior-secondary needs. Tutor selection should match the learner's current level rather than relying only on broad subject knowledge.

Common requests include Mathematics, Science, English, Hindi, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies, Computer Science and coding. Language, computer and hobby learning may also be available. Browse the subject tuition hub, then state the exact syllabus and topics when enquiring.

Yes, tutor requests may be made for CBSE, ICSE and State Board syllabi. Availability depends on the subject, class and location. Mention the full board name and current textbook or syllabus because similarly named chapters can differ in sequence, depth and examination style across boards.

Home tuition offers in-person instruction at an agreed location and may suit learners who benefit from direct supervision. Online tuition removes travel and can widen tutor choice. The better mode depends on attention needs, device access, subject, schedule, location and the student's comfort with digital learning.

A demo or introductory session may be available where offered by the tutor and suitable for the enquiry. It is not guaranteed in every case. Before attending, agree on whether the session is free or paid, its duration and purpose. Use it to assess clarity, preparation, listening and student participation.

TutorServices does not publish one universal fee because charges can vary by class, subject level, board, tutor experience, location, learning mode, session length and weekly frequency. The enquiry form lets families select hourly, monthly or per-course preferences and enter a Preferred fee (Per hour in Rupees) where relevant. Confirm the final amount before regular lessons.

The current student registration page states that submitting an enquiry and requesting a demo does not require an online registration payment. This does not mean all lessons or demos are free. Ask for written clarity about teaching fees, any applicable introductory-session charge and the payment arrangement before regular classes begin.

Confirm the agreed rate, fee structure, session duration, billing period, due date, accepted payment method, missed-class policy and notice requirements before continuing. TutorServices does not present one universal payment arrangement for every tutor. If any term is unclear, contact the team before sending money or starting a recurring schedule.

Contact TutorServices promptly and explain the specific concern, such as subject mismatch, communication, timing or locality. An alternative match may be reviewed subject to tutor availability and the enquiry details. An immediate replacement cannot be guaranteed, so provide constructive information that helps the team understand what needs to change.

Matching time varies with subject difficulty, class level, board, location, mode, timing and fee expectations. A common subject with flexible online timings may have more options than a specialised in-person request in a limited area. TutorServices does not promise a fixed matching deadline; complete details usually reduce avoidable delays.

Agree on measurable goals, lesson frequency, homework expectations and review intervals. Ask the tutor to explain covered topics, recurring errors, attendance and next steps. Progress reports or assessments may form part of the learning plan where arranged. Marks alone should not be the only measure; confidence, accuracy and independent work also matter.

No. Academic results depend on many factors, including attendance, prior knowledge, practice, health, examination difficulty and consistent effort. A tutor can provide explanation, structure, feedback and practice, but cannot ethically guarantee marks, ranks, selection or admission. Any such claim should be treated cautiously and clarified before payment.
Tutor Questions

Registration, profile review and teaching enquiries

Tutor registration allows a profile to be considered for relevant requirements; it is not an employment offer or a guarantee of student leads.

Complete the tutor registration form with accurate contact, qualification, experience, subject, board, class, teaching mode, location, fee and availability information. Upload a current resume. Registration submits your profile for review; it does not automatically approve the profile or guarantee an enquiry.

The form requests full name, mobile number, email, qualification, experience, subject expertise, boards taught, classes taught, preferred mode, preferred location, fee structure, Preferred fee (Per hour in Rupees), available timing and a resume. Complete, specific information supports better matching than broad claims such as “all subjects.”

Suitability depends on the subject and student level. Tutors should state their genuine academic qualifications and only select classes or subjects they can teach confidently. TutorServices does not publish one universal degree requirement for every category. Relevant educational background, current subject knowledge and clear communication may all be considered.

A tutor may submit accurate information even with limited experience, but profile consideration depends on the requirement and overall suitability. Do not exaggerate experience. New tutors should clearly describe subject strength, relevant qualifications, preferred class levels and any mentoring, tutoring or instructional work that can be represented truthfully.

Relevant submitted information may be reviewed for completeness and fit, including qualifications, experience, subjects, boards, classes, location, mode, schedule and resume details. Additional information may be requested when appropriate. Read the transparent tutor verification process for the scope and limitations of current review.

No universal identity, address or police background verification is claimed for every tutor. Additional documents or checks may be requested depending on circumstances and available processes. Tutors should provide truthful information, and families should complete any independent document or safety checks they consider necessary before confirming home lessons.

Review time can vary with profile completeness, document clarity, current requirements and whether further information is needed. TutorServices does not publish a guaranteed approval timeline. Keep your contact details active and respond accurately if clarification is requested. Repeated submissions with inconsistent information can delay a meaningful review.

No. Registration or profile approval does not guarantee leads, students, income or employment. Enquiries depend on current demand and compatibility with subject, class, board, location, mode, timing and fee expectations. Tutors should treat registration as an opportunity to be considered for relevant matches, not as a job appointment.

Choose the preferred structure—hourly, monthly or per course—and enter a realistic amount. The form uses the wording Preferred fee (Per hour in Rupees) for hourly expectations. Final terms may depend on class level, travel, session duration and frequency. Confirm the agreed fee and payment terms before teaching begins.

Yes, tutors can state their genuine preferred mode and location. Select only options you can support reliably. Home tuition requires realistic travel limits and punctuality, while online tuition requires stable internet, suitable equipment and confidence using digital teaching tools. Keeping availability current helps prevent unsuitable introductions.

Review the class, board, subject and requested topic. Prepare a short explanation, one diagnostic question and a suitable practice example. Ask about the learner's current difficulties and goals. If a demo is offered, confirm its duration and payment status in advance. Avoid promising marks or guaranteed results.

Use contact, academic and location information only for the relevant tuition arrangement. Do not share it publicly or use it for unrelated promotion. Communicate professionally, respect family boundaries and obtain appropriate parental involvement for minors. Contact TutorServices if an enquiry appears misleading, unsafe or substantially different from the stated requirement.

Learning modes, locations and programme availability

Explore the service scope, then use the relevant page or contact route for your exact requirement.

TutorServices connects students with available tutors in supported locations, with current location content focused on Delhi and areas such as South Delhi, Dwarka, Rohini and Connaught Place. Coverage is not guaranteed in every neighbourhood. Availability depends on class, subject, timetable, fee preference and a tutor's practical travel range.

Online tuition can be requested from across India because lessons do not require local travel. A suitable match still depends on subject, board, class, language, schedule and available tutors. Students need a reliable internet connection, a usable device and a quiet learning space for consistent participation.

The services hub covers home tuition, online tuition, offline tuition, one-to-one tuition, group tuition, academic coaching, computer courses and hobby classes. Availability varies by tutor, subject and location. Choose the format according to learner independence, need for supervision, interaction preference, budget and schedule.

One-to-one tuition is an individual lesson between one tutor and one learner. It allows pace, examples and practice to respond to the student's needs. It may suit learners with specific gaps or focused goals, but results still require attendance and practice. Individual tuition can take place at home or online.

Group tuition combines structured teaching with peer questions and shared practice, often at a lower per-student cost. Individual tuition offers more personalised pacing and attention. Group learning works best when learners have compatible levels and goals. Ask about batch size, level, interaction, assessment and missed-class arrangements before joining.

Foundation and preparation support may be requested for JEE, NEET, CUET and Olympiad pathways. The service focuses on concept development, practice, doubt solving, revision and study planning. It does not guarantee examination rank, score, selection or admission. State the exam, current class, target year and required subjects when enquiring.

Yes, learners may request Spoken English and English grammar support. Spoken English focuses on practical communication, confidence, pronunciation and conversation, while grammar classes focus on sentence structure, accuracy and writing. Programmes can support students and adult learners, but no guaranteed fluency, marks, interview success or job placement is promised.

Computer learning may include fundamentals, MS Office, internet and email skills, digital productivity, school Computer Science support, Scratch, coding foundations and beginner Python, depending on instructor availability. Mention the learner's age, current skill level, device access and goal so the tutor can recommend an appropriate starting point.

Hobby enquiries may include drawing, sketching, painting, music, dance, arts and crafts, public speaking, creative writing, chess, abacus and personality development. Availability varies significantly by location, mode and instructor expertise. Confirm the age group, materials, class format, safety arrangements and learning expectations before starting.

Yes. A request can focus on one subject, a weak chapter, revision, homework support or a broader academic plan. Be specific about the required scope and expected duration. A short topic-based need may have different scheduling and fee terms from continuous year-round tuition, so confirm the arrangement clearly.

A small group request may be possible when learners have compatible classes, subjects, boards, levels and schedules. Sharing a tutor does not automatically mean identical needs. Discuss batch size, lesson pace, individual doubt time, assessment and total fees. The arrangement remains subject to a suitable tutor accepting the group format.

A student generally needs a stable internet connection, a phone, tablet or computer with working audio, and a quiet study space. Some subjects benefit from a larger screen, digital writing tool or document camera. Confirm the meeting platform, file-sharing method and backup plan before the first lesson.
